# Copie privacy policy — draft

**Publisher:** [publisher name]  
**Contact:** [support email]  
**Last updated:** [publication date]

Copie is a clipboard history app for macOS. While it runs, it reads new content placed on the system clipboard and saves readable copies on your Mac so you can find and reuse them. History may include text, formatted text, images, file references, thumbnails, and full copies of image and regular files that fit within your chosen history limit. Clipboard content can include sensitive information, so use Copie only on a Mac and account you trust.

Copie does not send clipboard history to the publisher or a third-party server. The current app does not include analytics, advertising, or cloud sync. Its history is stored in the app's local Application Support directory. You can delete saved entries or clear history in Settings and change the storage limit. Removing the app does not automatically remove its history files.

Capture Area runs only when you choose it. macOS may request Screen Recording permission so Copie can capture the selected area. The captured image is copied to the clipboard and may be saved in history.

You can stop Copie from monitoring the clipboard by quitting the app. If you have questions about this policy or want help removing local data, contact [support email].
